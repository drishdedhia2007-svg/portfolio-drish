## The Brief

In 2023 I joined the virtual Mechanical Design Internship Program at STAR — Space Technology and Aeronautical Rocketry. Our team needed to turn a static motor test pad idea into something people could see and discuss. I worked on the mechanical concept in Fusion 360 and helped the team prepare a design proposal. My wider internship work also included Proteus.

At that stage, I was still learning what a CAD model needs to do. My immediate goal was to make our idea visible enough for a conversation, not to deliver a finished, buildable test rig.

## What I Made

I arranged structural profiles into a frame with circular motor-support features and produced views of the concept for the proposal. Seeing the idea as a 3D object helped us point to parts of the design instead of describing everything in words.

## The Mistake in Plain Sight

Several profiles overlap in the model. I placed members where I wanted the overall structure to be, but did not resolve exactly how they would join. That is my modeling shortcoming, not a detail to hide. The images do not establish workable connections, clearances, or structural performance, and I would not treat this as a fabrication drawing or a validated test stand.

If I revisited it, I would start by defining the interfaces and constraints, then model joints and fasteners deliberately. I would check clearances and loads before presenting a version as something that could be built or used. The project taught me the difference between **communicating a concept** and **engineering the physical system**.

## What Came Out of It

The concept did what our early proposal needed: it made an idea visible for the team and others to discuss. STAR's director later signed a letter of recommendation recognizing my participation, problem solving, and teamwork during the 24 June–24 July 2023 program. I value that recognition, while also seeing the overlaps and missing engineering in the model much more clearly now. That is exactly why I want this project in my portfolio.
